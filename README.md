## Introduction

Welcome to **Wal Aser** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

By using Wal Aser (the "App"), you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Location Data:** When you grant permission, we access your approximate or precise location to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction.
- **Custom Azan Files:** If you choose to set a custom Azan, the App accesses an audio file from your device storage. This file remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
- **Favorites & Bookmarks:** Your favorites, bookmarked ayahs, and reading preferences are stored locally on your device.

### 1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect
We do **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or sell:
- Your name, email, or phone number
- Your contacts, messages, or photos
- Your personal files or audio library
- Payment information (the App is free)
- Browsing history or usage analytics tied to your identity

### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information
- **Crash Reports & Diagnostics:** If the App crashes, anonymous crash data may be collected to fix bugs. This data does not identify you personally.
- **Device Information:** Basic device type (Android version, screen size) may be used to optimize the App's performance.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

- **Calculate Prayer Times:** Your location is used solely to compute accurate prayer times for your city.
- **Determine Qibla Direction:** Your location helps determine the direction to Kaaba.
- **Save Your Preferences:** Favorites, bookmarks, font settings, and language preferences are stored locally for a personalized experience.
- **Send Notifications:** Azan alerts, prayer reminders, and Islamic event notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
- **Improve the App:** Anonymous crash reports help us fix bugs and improve stability.

**We do NOT use your data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.**

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

### 3.1 Local Storage
- All your personal data (favorites, bookmarks, settings) is stored **locally on your device**.
- Downloaded books, azan files, and prayer data are stored in the App's private storage.
- We do not have access to this data.

### 3.2 Cloud Services
The App may use **Firebase Realtime Database** (provided by Google) to fetch:
- Islamic events data
- Prayer time calculation methods
- App update notifications

This data is **read-only** and contains no personal information about you.

### 3.3 Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

| Permission | Purpose |
